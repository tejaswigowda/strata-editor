Bind a face GLB to a VRM

Start from a photo. The page reads the face on your device, makes a face-blendshape GLB from it, and drives a base VRM's expression blendshapes from that GLB's morphs, and (optionally) moves the VRM's face shape, hair shape and colors toward the photo. The skeleton, skin weights and existing blendshapes are not changed, and the hairstyle is scaled and shifted, not replaced.

Stays on this device. Your photo, the face GLB made from it, and everything derived from them are processed in this page and are never sent to any server. The face model runs locally from files served with this page (nothing from a CDN). The page makes GET requests only, and the whole thing works with the network off once the page has loaded.
Approximate, not a likeness guarantee. The binding makes the VRM's expressions drivable from the face source. The shape and color matching below is a rough, measured nudge (eye size and spacing, mouth, jaw, profile depth; skin, hair, eye and brow color); an anime base will still look like an anime character.
